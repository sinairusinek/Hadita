## THE ROW-ALIGNMENT TRAP (cost three false measurements this session)

The proxy GT was transcribed against **final2** rows; final3 changed row counts.
Worse, **on p4 and p5 the GT carries a leading carry-forward row** — the tail of
an entry begun on the previous page, money columns only, no Serial_No — that the
grid does not have. So GT row 0 is grid row -1 there.

I got this wrong three times, each time producing a fake failure rate:
Needleman-Wunsch over row signatures (18.8% miss, p9/p10 at 88%/74%), then
top-anchored index (7.7%), then serial-anchored (**3.5%**, correct).

**Always anchor on Serial_No** — `best_offset()` in `push_gt_to_final3.py`.
It matches 34/34 serials at -1 on p4, 29/29 at -1 on p5, 0 elsewhere.

`score_exp2608.py` is NOT affected: `pairs_by_offset` detects the offset itself
(it independently finds +1 on p4/p5), so **the survey numbers above stand**.
